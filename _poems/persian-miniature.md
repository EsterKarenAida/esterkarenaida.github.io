Whole colonies of peonies, each a throng of wings,
descend to the earth’s upturned hands,
opening their hearts til translucence.
At dusk the scent of their crushed petals is
unbearable. And still morn will come; we
search the clouds for them as if for a nascent
Dalai Lama. An aroma of pavement
rises, it is my unwashed legs. All that
steamrolled me, weighted by backhoes and semis
-can it hide the newly-born? My limbs are wide,
I stink in the sun, and still I hope my life
won’t step on anyone else’s life. It can’t be.
-How can it not be? The world feels a tree-root
cracking pavement, rising like a wisdom-tooth.
