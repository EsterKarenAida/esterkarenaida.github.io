taking the sum of memories -

the slenderest canes laid away in their quiver-
weaving I-don’t-yet-know-what- of them;

are they a doll? are they a basket

Miryam will watch over, her brother inside,
knowing she must create something

so watertight, it will protect him

as she pushes him off a bit,
to allow the river in turn to carry him,

this dip and swell will buoy him  - her eyes

all hope - to see a far picture,
and others will help - the hope in her fingers

skimming like a jesus bug, skimming like the hook

to draw him home, this time, more light
and again
