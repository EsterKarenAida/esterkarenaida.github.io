Gamliel pops, was he adrift in that sea, and forgot
Jerusalem?
Wild child, could be you’ve struck the whole Earth is full of His Glory
by the western route

for when brothers in learning set sail, one was asleep and awake,
        one awake, and asking, what startled you?
Did you see the ocean’s creatures by the glow of one pearl?

We came with our tongue, an entire face to the world.
        We say, let’s make art of Jerusalem, see what spawns.
I am starving for these conversations. Like a pendulum, like a
        guillotine.

Its single blade of grass. A serpent with a flash of green in its mouth;
        it might heal.

They say, ekphrastics, so often, from paintings; I am possessed
        by the Bridge of Strings without water.

It’s another way I thought your cup was mine; now
        I want to drain it.
It will widen to the bed of a sea where I rush to seep into
        warm pebbles and fall back against my will to the dark,
to the seething, west and east, west and east from here I cannot
orient myself.

I fly to the moon-anchor, a great round clock-face in Davidson Tower,
        the lump of fat under the buffalo’s coarse pelt. I mix all the
numbers up, yet still, I dwindle.

I search for stems of grass between sandy patches of rock; if my hide
becomes a kayak, let me burst against a hidden cliff,
let the dam spurt high. I will graze at the phosphorescent sea-floor where
the light pools.

I bless you to see watery distorted gulls convening,
and even the fishes shall crowd to hear you at the great
submerged bedrock, the pierced place, the well from which heart
-soul is drawn.
