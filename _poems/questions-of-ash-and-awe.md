*Canst thou bind the chains of the Pleiades,*
*or loosen the cords of Orion?…*
*Knowest thou the ordinances of the Heavens?*

**I**

We have become orphans and fatherless… little children faint for thirst in the streets.

How the City sits, solitary, that was full of people!

A grapevine fettered itself to the inner wall of the kitchen, my one hundred years of solitude.

For what exactly do we sit in huts that rattle in the wind, recalling a sacred Pillar of cloud?

Pour out your heart like water before the Face of the Lord.

What remains?

As in water face answers to face, so the heart of man to man.

What remains?

How the City sits, solitary.

**II**

Let him sit alone and keep silence.

Wait, is this any way to console the bereaved?

Let him offer his cheek to him who strikes him.

Angels, did they slander you, saying you were singing when God’s children were drowning?

Hasida, Hasida, with your white beak, will Jerusalem yet remember us?

How the City sits, solitary.

**III**

Why should you care?

Why am I here? Where are we going?

What does it matter?

I need a sixth sense, non-verbal, to tune in to my teen.

How the City sits, solitary.

**IV**

Is it normal that I am a problem to myself?

How did your parents raise you kids to be so service-oriented?

What possible link in the near-extinction of Indian Sandalwood, Swallow Root, whooping cranes, the El Molo?

What’s the difference between movable and non-movable property?

How the City sits, solitary.

**V**

How do you conceive of equality in the Eyes of God?

Do we have a share in solitary confinement?

One who recognizes all men as members of his own body is a sound man to guard them.

Why are Native Americans and people of color more likely to have underlying health conditions?

How the City sits, solitary.

**VI**

Handle a large kingdom with as gentle a touch as if you were cooking small fish.

Why don’t weeds need to be watered?

Do you mean that during this year of Rest for the Land, I can harvest Neil’s mustard greens? But he can’t dig them out of his pepper bed?

Take rest: a field that has rested gives a bountiful crop.

By what grace are Texas Bluebonnets now covering the Hill of Evil Counsel?
