---
title: Sonnet at the Cross
opening: So unguarded and marshlike, this border
drafted: November 2017
note: From the poems she had finished but not yet published.
---

So unguarded and marshlike, this border,
I crossed it in my sleep. I had the
complete documentation necessary,
I turned on my phone light — everything fled
in the tiny floodlight but the letters
streaming from the stabilo. It’s all
organic matter now on the other
side except these one or two lines — that I
thought of you there — whether through fate or sheer
force of my intent. Slay, sue, detain me.
I got out bleeding a little at my
feet, to tell you, you are real to me in
all countries. And here your passport, your cool,
your feet up like a day at the beach.
