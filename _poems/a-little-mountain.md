---
title: A Little Mountain
opening: When you say I saw Shubert
manuscript: Centrifuge Rattle
note: >-
  From the poems she had finished but not yet published. Transcribed from her
  typescript.
---

When you say I saw Shubert
Or we saw some banjo out in Moravia
I think of the mama of all synesthesia
over at Sinai
and smile

thankful for the ethnic strains, booth to booth,
backsound on the Mount,
now Yemenite or Chinese

the way you hear me,
my poems clamoring for your eyes

Lo, your kokopeli cell that calls me
on its own, showing me your dash
or you absently biting your nails
against sky sheets
streaming egg - drop ribbons,
scattered apple petals
