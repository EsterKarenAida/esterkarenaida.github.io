Ana, rachmana: liba bidna litzlan
please accept me for I am always pidgin

I am the song of David and Devora,
Miriam and Moses, Rumi and Paul Robeson

Willingly I entered the womb of Deir Yassin,
Willingly I amble over the stones of a Jewish asylum.

What can I offer you?
And what has song ever done for humanity?

I heard your voice in the rain
I heard your voice in the old-new tones of the loving

and the penitent in Maryland, Amsterdam, Jerusalem.
I reflect you now in this tenuous rainbow

from Jabel Mukaber to Ramat Rachel, beyond a grove of carob.
In stealth I gathered up spent rose-petals, without knowing

what new hope You would offer, nor the number of days
that would cycle and whirl like petals.
