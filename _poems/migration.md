Great wave, will I find ease in you?

Fearfully made, cloud-moving sea,

waves of ships spraying the harbor at Haifa,

Nisei crowding the shores of Vancouver,

let me hear the voice of your experience?

What particles of sediment, pearl,

sound, or memory do you bear?

“I smoked some weed once”, the rabbi said,

“with this old guy. He says to me,

‘You look for the high. But me,

I take it in now, I just ride the waves.’ “

I think of the skiffs In Hokusai’s woodcut,

A few corn-husk dolls in a torrent. Hard to notice,

Starred Danish kids in dark fishing-boats to Sweden,
numbering the stars.

By what agency did our minds survive

A history teacher’s experiment in Palo Alto?

Obersalzberg, what was your nature

Before you were drowned by power?

What flame-leafed finger, what parchment,

would poke above the roiling mass

to grant rest to the wings of a stork?
