---
title: stop watching the movie
opening: it was so beautiful
drafted: July 2018
note: From the poems she had finished but not yet published.
---

it was so beautiful
and then so hard I felt I could not bear it
there are so many discrete instances this applies to
I don’t know which to tell you

you said or someone said or there were
words hanging in the air

(in a naugahyde couch in a corner of the cavernous hospital lobby
faint strains of impossible pipe organ)

the words said
come back to your own life
just for one day
stop watching the movie
(your eye hungry to read as a student of semantics)

I am trying Lord
to let the love that adopted me with the heart of this new mother reach me
but I manage sometimes only to
remember to breathe

we figured it was about thirty or so times
my dr and I had done this
so why were we reminding each other
before the preliminary steps of
five needles of local
followed by the transducer

We have a system, I tell him there is
dull ache and there is sharp (easier
to understand while he works than
‘acute’) so I don’t bother telling you
about dull ache, that’s normal, I will
tell you if sharp.
got it, he says. are u painful now?
no you’re good
guttural as eloquent
and to some cultures uncouth to give voice
surprised me, acute on my right side
that hasn’t happened before
I can’t quite avoid telling u when it’s sharp
it’s involuntary
realize
we are reading intakes of breath here

I am breathing and slowly and tears
stream down so I prepare myself, if I
should have to reply,
I am crying for everything but the experience of the biopsy
so many times what was outside of my skin has penetrated completely within my body
that I feel completely vulnerable in these moments

a friend said
your presence is all that we need
and that made me feel so supported I didn’t know what to say

but am sometimes terrified to be present to myself — alone
heart of my heart, I said the wrong thing,
I need you very much like some animal with ratified diet; I thrive in your light.
