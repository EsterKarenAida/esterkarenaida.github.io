Sun pours onto the watertower in Gilo,
over St John's  monastery, runs down the alley

to wink back in sunflowers at a cafe table--
onto the Vizhnitz Talmud Torah;

and inward of Hadassah's glass case. Like chocolates,
my spanking, boxed contagion.

*

From Jerusalem to Hevron is the distance
from a hand extended, to the two chambers of a heart.

Four popped veins later.
74 shot over the weekend in Chicago,

Mi yemalel and who will number Gaza,
or you, Hevron and  Etzion.

So many uses for a carton, a dovecote,
a tomb, with rows of dark openings.
Breathing at both ends, it becomes a flute.

What is it that sickens about a symphonic
score set before a firing range?

and I all puffy-eyed after one night
missing their lights around my table.

   *

the body sleeps but the heart awakens
to your bleating, Khalil.

May all that you need, like right rain
shine from your streets.

this prattle only: drops on your leaves, bread enough,
bee-balm and jasmine.

A prayer in stillness, where are we?
-why are we here?

sometimes it’s Jerusalem’s petals,
parchment- thin,  a hand to lift the heart of Khalil.

today Khalil sends two doctoras, one
praying with me, while the other heals me, finally

-- through the vein in my hand; her oath of kindness;

and the Yidden who come all the way to the end of this floor to sound the shofar:

 a mikveh of healing water in the clarion rinsing within us.

Through Your Grace, Avinu, blessed Holy One, escape Your  tower, greet us in field and home and shul; please bend to us, lift us, carry us.
