We are one ancient stone skull
perforated by sockets, ears, nostrils,
all, a kind of mouth

We are endless pockets
the gloves I passed in a dream
to the veiled woman on the train
-she crowded them into her coat pocket
with her own, so many warming layers
waiting to emerge

We are origami prayer-bits half-out
of hundreds of smooth or wrinkled lips

We are walking mumbled prayers
in bright scarves, yellow baseball caps,
fedoras and boots (folded together willy-nilly
like homework in a child’s pack)
in the smooth aluminium pocket
of the light rail

Let these the pulsating,
each with their own truth
graffitti my pocked brow

Let the human, the taper of light
called after My imprint,
offer themselves to any of my seven mouths
in something like a kiss

They may flicker, yet remain,
One part heart (chiseled to be shelter)
One part flame-in-pocket, divine pocket,
a business card, a name, a seeing soul,
speaking one another.
