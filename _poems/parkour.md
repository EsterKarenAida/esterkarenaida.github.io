All she needs is a silver-shot dress and
my daughter’s off, pirouetting through the
salon. Did you see the clip of Ahmed?
He teaches Parkour in Sweden, learned by
leaping Gaza’s torn walls. Ali tells in
his father’s name, the One who put your head
on your shoulders will be the One to take
It off.
When I heard it my mind split to Rabbi
Eliezer, who told his son at ten,
Nothing to fear but the one Above.
Like this season’s flu, when a spirit-germ
is born, it’s everywhere, the Soncino
or the Chinese printing-press, but, please God,
this one’s in our guts for good. Kamenetz
maintains Rebbe Nachman returned as the soul
of Kafka to appeal to us. You want
your horses to neigh, says the Besht, you ease
up on the reins.
To love with grit is more than singing, more
at trusting and sweating; if you have a good
horse, you don’t need the whip.
 I’m neighing.
I want to be worthy of your leap.
