The day lacks fullness if I have not
recorded something;
no swipe at all
through my soul’s chimney.

I ask my teacher: have you heard
of cases where angels came or a
prophecy to the mouth, since Avraham,
for sake of consolation, solely?

One reason Muhammad was blessed
with Sura Yusuf, all-of-a-piece, was to console him
after the deaths of his uncle
and his beloved

Khadijah. Here is another who suffered like you.
How we need to be called;
How we are drawn to the frog, chirping
in spring
the beloved voice

reviving us after a season we feared.
I scare myself. Poets, artists, tell me,
does Spirit speak most through you after
the death of those dear?
Does She attempt
to comfort you when you are cold, brick, hollow?
