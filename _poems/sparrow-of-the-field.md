Look at the family pictures, begin to feel
Fine root-hairs outstretched in the soft earth
share nourishment and what they know, trees
a little more encircling for the one all underground

A widow too young, she wants to hear his voice
All of his overspilling love for humanscape earth
Again. Dum spiro spero.
While we live, we hope, and longer.

I’m a dumb bird here
We don’t talk much anymore
But sometimes hear one another in acts
Or photos light sends to rest on earth.

We ask permission
What is more private an angel domain
than a secret name?
There to reflect hidden souls and larks.

Lord, tell his love while she sleeps,
Should it be Your will.
Eavesdrop in the dark of night on Shabbat
(try to distinguish wind from earth?)

“Sparrow of the field” comes and
I can’t sleep, can’t write, but want to recall.
In the kitchen I find a small plaster-dove candlestick
white as Noh. Set it on the green ground

Of a Christmas card photo of a remembered
path through the turning leaves. For morning.
I will soak here in lemongrass-scented water
forever. Never thirsting, in pocked earth.

Where is the handle, where the door?
Is it possible to search without knowing what you
search for? All search fields lead to flocks
who don’t need even a barn from the rain-

Build your barn of love and if you like
I will help you. I feel so many people in pain
triggered here in the water. I feel my right finger
spasm, wanting one instant of power

and expression. G-d, don’t put into my hand
easy tools of destruction. Let me see my
triggers coming, let me be strong enough
to name them, let their metal weight be eased

from crushing me. I see that there is death
in Your world. I hear it crying, a population
of moments turning rigid
as the rocky ground of Ireland

or Beer Sheva. Let death’s tears
wet the ground so something can grow,
something good to eat? Maybe we’ll speak
of ravens, because you want to tell of

the black and redemptive showing the way
out of the cages? The dark night as freedom’s
cradle. As Jews, torn, Listen: wrest; be one
flame from earth-dark night.
