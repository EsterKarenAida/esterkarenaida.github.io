---
title: I Have Trouble Seeing the Perseids
opening: Could be, you could have helped me through
manuscript: Centrifuge Rattle
note: >-
  From the poems she had finished but not yet published. Her own note gives the
  source of one line, “In the future even the stones will need one another,”
  which is Trịnh Công Sơn. The section numbers are hers.
---

I

Could be, you could have helped me through
crisis after crisis. I turned away.
Continents apart, what can we do?
Just give me an opening like a snagged thread,
a mustard seed.
- And still, did I learn this long love from you?

One Hell is just this,
no opening. Look up- do you see those holes,
the pinpricks of light that filigree the
universe? A trickle’s turned to steady,
spilling, luminous ocean. God begins to
show the suffering She hid, faithful and unnamed,
yearning to console Abraham.

II

You sent up sky-lanterns, pictographs
in the night that Fritz passed. Our hearts
churn with questions; aching, we cling to the fur of what is
so vast, and the starry lights, to comfort
in our blindness.
O Logan Airport
at 5 AM- is there a place as lonely?
Blinking boards of flights I do not understand,
as Blackbird Singing in the Dead of Night
floats like pollen from the intercom.

What came before?
The grains of sand, blue-flamed to glass?
Pocasset, Penacook, Massachusetts.

III

A rawhide mandala to drape the ground
for dance, all speaking-eyed silence in Fruitlands Museum.
When my cloistered mind gropes at
how God speaks to us in patterns, from Her
aerial view, I also see: the sun-burst,
the feathered spokes of the chief's head-dress.
We offer the Holy One the constellation of
ourselves, each stiff quill; the round table so like yours,
from whence our thoughts take wing. Circle of
Algonquin democracy. Clearing at the
center. Do not step there, it is human
ground.

IV

With such layered constellations,
I have trouble placing the texture I see on your screen,
behind the icons. It seems microscopic—
yet, you say, a map of the galaxies,
the reassuring, distressing tracks of refugee God.

She thought to tell us of Her trek in us, soul-tired,
alley-weary, She thought to tell us,
like a child lost among so many
distinguished pant-legs, her feet scratching characters
in endless sand.
In the future even the stones will need one another.

V

From the small plane, looking down upon the cities,
I could not see if crowds of light bordered water or mountain;
both were threadbare openings against black.
For love, I saw only a light-studded field.

I have trouble seeing the Perseids
after the day of my eyes
has mirrored your city beside me.
