title: Stop, Thief
opening: you forgot a pocket

you forgot a pocket
left inside jacket pocket

out of courtesy
if you were to leave
in place of my words

one of yr own poems

(I crave your art,
your long stories
so much I begin
to croon to them)

Paradox, welcome.
Haida, Anasazi.

Seraph or monkey-fiend!
Stele-like etched
cornices mimicking us,

tossed drafts: respecting now
the toxicity of chemicals.
meadows of origin

at slant-light dawn, mitosis
in crimson and speckled blue,
cold stalls for watchmen,

any oval table if you are there,
burned books that are
not consumed

and those we love,
the impossibly long

arrow dyeing and blossoming
my heart from France,

its head, its long body a hundred
clementines in drafty winter.

the Japanese show us
the harvest moon:

the clear light allows us
to gather into the night,
gather well enough

to feed a merry company and live
for months on the delight left over:

as my therapist puts it: the task
of the second half of life is to
understand the first;

you are in me
a three-fold cord not
easily frayed or cut.

If you were
out of courtesy to leave
one of yours — as often

when we speak
I’m of three minds, one heart,
tied tongue —

the next scene might best
be left for the big screen.
O strange

meet cute like a shopping cart
and bike toppled and chained
together along the light rail,

Every time I open my mouth
your lines would well
like trimmed wicks

from my circumcised
heart
