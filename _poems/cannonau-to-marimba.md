---
title: Cannonau to Marimba
opening: I try on a lavender down coat Mom brings
note: >-
  From the poems she had finished but not yet published. The poem is set at
  Purim, at the reading of the Book of Esther, in the first months of the
  pandemic.
---

I try on a lavender down coat Mom brings
and the ballerina informs me i look like a blue nun
and someone said, this too will pass

Or a jacket, red with cheetah print lining,
these spots everywhere: dalmation scarf
or leopard lily on the patio; on the Fitness cereal box

a river of honeyed flakes, little continents
of dark cacao darting like fishes.
Sweet intermissions of light.

Velvet night of lupine with its flash of white.
You recall a treatment for lupus, off-label, will help
with the immune balance we need for Corona.
A time of restraint, Esther does not mention G-d

the way a love letter might not mention love.
The story of man, spotted with our accidents of Grace,
an encoded pattern. Menahem reads Esther from herstory unfurled,

his marked up with all the hidden names of God.
Just before sunset he'll read at Steve and Andrea's,
i wanna be in that number, hopi for a little jazz &amp; falling water.
