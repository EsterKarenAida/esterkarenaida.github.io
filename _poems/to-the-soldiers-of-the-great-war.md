  From the poems she had finished but not yet published. Her own notes: “Yom

If I had only made you love me, I could not have saved you.

If I had only made you love me, I could not have saved you.

Just out of Jerusalem, God has speared our hearts
like gold onions perched at the roof of the cathedral
and ready to jump.

Yom Asel, Yom Basel. One heart still here said:
“We will keep knocking on the doors of your hearts in joy,”
but I will bless the Czar as he blesses us all.
These are my principles. If you don’t like them, I have others.

And you, the dayanim in that little tower where
they lose life at the bottom of the archives
and then retrieve it again, or it struts in when it is no longer
between eight and one pm,
speak up. Tell these expectant faces the necessary story
of how I came back from the dead to see my children live.
Tell and retell them that living for your children is better
than dying for them, for at times we are afraid
to hear good news.

Let’s test it out: the pussycat director of Combatants for Peace
told me, sure you can join us.
Reliable sources say, demonstrate for something, not against,
so I will in fact terrorize them, just teach me how, as it’s a skill
anyone can learn, and my battery will miraculously last on empty
for as long as it takes to get my little drama queen home.
We talked about my personality change, yeah, I guess
holding an Arab heart in Jewish history will cause some
twinning and twining hutzpah in the DNA.

In the same week I asked COGAT if I could teach English for them
but my request must be lost in a paper graveyard.
I would be so good at it, they wouldn’t know what hit them.
But maybe they think poets are delicate?
I’m a mommy, they could be imagining i don’t know how to yell.
It just won’t work to distance-teach English for COGAT through
Skype because the land favors no one, the wind laughs on internet,
and makes my students freeze. So the following are my demands:

Car (and driver)
No more than 40-minute commute from Jerusalem each way
No more than twice weekly unless the work is positively energizing.

O ye of little faith.
Haven’t we already had a trial period?
Salary negotiable but all the books I need; outside students at my discretion.
Find out where you will get Kahan’s natural hazelnut syrup
in this God-forsaken country, it’s the only way to sweeten coffee.
— You. Soldier, with the smirk. You think I’m joking?
Make a choice, ya’ala maher, we can’t all be grandmas at the ATM.
It’s either a little sweetness or a rock garden in perfect rows
right outside my window.
Ladies, I don’t want to hear with the coffee,
ooh i really like your glasses, HaMefakedet.
Just come back to me after this course
and tell me you saved your partner.

There is a man in Illinois who says he wishes he could help
but dislikes our healthy noise. Yet if he were to dig up
the stories of warring Greek city-states in my cold ear
and tell me why I want to know, then I alone will set my teeth
to the stone Eucharist, and we can all sleep as the meek.

You don’t know what in Sam Hill I’m talking about, do you?

MI AMAR AD MATAI?
