---
title: Carousel-Pipes
opening: White gold on warm, the band my father
drafted: September 2017
note: From the poems she had finished but not yet published.
---

**I**

White gold on warm, the band my father
gave my mother. I try it on, hope it will prevent
questions I don’t want to answer.

As I replace the books on their shelves
it flashes. Could be my mother keeps
the curtains she sewed to soften that one hour

each day the sun blinded the sitting room.
What’s to be done with this band like a truncated
flute?

**II**

Begin to breathe the story through a round
lens. The children will look there if they choose.
I was angry she did not prepare me.

With one restless finger I twist and trace the
seamless, searching, all my life.
Band, belt, once-liquid metal, contain breath

for music. As breath I have passed through
and fanned out: a scent of his carousel-pipes; mulched
years of sweet mown grass to veil my mother’s eyes.
