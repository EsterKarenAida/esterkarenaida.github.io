How will I know the number of days it
takes wisps of ash, streaked sea-fur, to enact
their vaporous cycle that pinpricks my
cheeks? My father must be a little more
at peace now, the soft weight, his play on my
hair, a new blessing. Why then this leaking,
nose, eyes, mouth? Voyage. China. So fragile,
why? -Who will rage you love? Jerusalem
is better than Jerusalem the way
the risk of a moment’s tears and  disdain,
my heart’s heat, is better than any
decorous ditty I can drag in. Hold
everything. Kazantzakis, lover of
all life, I am so afraid. I hoped to
see the road with you again that neither
of us has seen before. We should insist
while there is still time.
