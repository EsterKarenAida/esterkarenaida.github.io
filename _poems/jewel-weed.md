I wanted to show you the site of the explosion
        and the way a few deft projectiles, at length
 in a drift like milkweed, lost momentum

I wanted to allow your sense of a dense aroma, light-tight as a pod
        beside a vessel, amber with weak tea
disoriented jasmine

What, though yielding, yet remains?

I want to tell you how your presence
        combing out a child’s tangles
        loosened the locks

There, now. Somebody’s pocket-knife half-buried
alongside the road. With all this rain we’re having
soon it will rust

To the earth. To show you a hedgerow of brushes erect as lavender,
tips color-sharp, and orange jewel-weed in ditches- some call it
Touch-me-Not. Let the rains drench them to life.

The wild knowing ones show where they will veer
by their will alone.

Let me be still enough
a pool, yet fluid and clear, to reflect
your disarming unfurling, vital as peat, an alighting like an intake of air.
